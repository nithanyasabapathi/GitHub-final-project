# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Do not engage in harassment or personal attacks.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement
Inappropriate behavior may be reviewed by project maintainers. They may take appropriate corrective action based on the circumstances.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers will review reports fairly, document relevant details, and take proportionate action.

## Attribution
This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
